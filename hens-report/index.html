Location 1: RRR (main)

Location 1: RRR, each outcome relative to total purchases

The main results: each outcome against total purchases, so a shift between outcomes rather than a change in how much was sold. Top row all dishes, with the simple pre/post comparison; bottom row entrees. A1 counts daily purchases; A2 is the customer model, on the additive scale, so its tiles are in items per order rather than ratios. Expand a tile with its button (Escape closes it); click a point for that fit's prediction figure, or a pre/post diamond for the same figure with the prediction replaced by the pre/post averages. The buttons download each figure as a PDF. Marked rows: † marks the fits with divergent transitions: A1 entrees target (Chicken roll) (1), A1 entrees vegan (47, kept as fitted by decision). Each black diamond is a simple before/after comparison with no model, on level rows only: the average over the open days (A1) or days (A2, the day-level model: each day's orders averaged, as that model fits them) after an introduction against the average before it, from the rows the fit trained on and on the scale of the tile it sits in. Its black bar is one standard error either side and its grey bar the 95% t interval, with Welch-Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, drawn just under the model's own estimate on the same row. A1 is the ratio of the two average daily counts, its standard error the delta method's on the log scale; A2 is Welch's two-sample t test on those daily averages. Against total purchases (A1 RRR, A2 difference), the outcome's comparison and total's are combined as independent, as the model's contrast takes its two fits. Windows (equal: as many days before as after, neither crossing the other introduction): Chicken roll, 23 Mar 2026 to 28 Apr 2026 against 29 Apr 2026 to 4 Jun 2026; Dragon wrap, A1 29 Apr 2026 to 4 Jun 2026 against 5 Jun 2026 to 11 Jul 2026, A2 3 May 2026 to 4 Jun 2026 against 5 Jun 2026 to 7 Jul 2026.
