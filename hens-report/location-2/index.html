Location 2: RRR (main)

Location 2: RRR, each outcome relative to total purchases

  • RRR: an outcome's RR ÷ total purchases' RR, i.e. the change in its share. 0.97 = share fell 3%.
  • Layout: top row all dishes, bottom row entrees.
  • Interaction: click a point for that fit's prediction figure; expands a tile (Esc closes); the buttons download PDFs.
  • Pre/post (diamonds, all dishes): a simple before/after comparison, no model. It compares the average per open day after the launch with the average before it, over the fit's training days. A1 is the ratio of the two averages. Black bar ±1 SE, grey bar 95% t interval (Welch–Satterthwaite). Click a diamond for the figure with the window averages in place of the prediction.
  • Equal windows (as many days before as after): Black Sheep 3 May–17 Oct 2021 vs 18 Oct 2021–3 Apr 2022.
